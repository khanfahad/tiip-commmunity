A note on scope. TIIP training is intended for those who are legally and ethically permitted to practice psychotherapy or mental-health counseling in their own jurisdiction. It is not a qualification for life-coaching, imam or chaplaincy guidance, or other non-clinical roles, and it does not by itself authorize anyone to practice psychotherapy.

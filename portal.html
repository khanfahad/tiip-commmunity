A note on scope. TIIP training is intended for those who are legally and ethically permitted to practice psychotherapy or mental-health counseling in their own jurisdiction. It is not a qualification for life-coaching, imam or chaplaincy guidance, or other non-clinical roles, and it does not by itself authorize anyone to practice psychotherapy.

🔐 Level 1 — Foundations Curriculum

The Level 1 modules: the foundations of TIIP, the role of the TIIP clinician, assessment & conceptualization, the elements of the psyche — ʿaql, nafs, iḥsās, rūḥ — and Islamic virtues. Work through every module, then complete the required TIIP Case Formulation Sheet — the end-of-level case conceptualization, reviewed by an admin — who passes it or asks you to retry — before Level 1 is credited.

🔐 Level 1 enrolment Admin only

Level 1 is not listed publicly and is invisible to Level 0 members by default. Enrolling a member makes them a Level 1 trainee: the modules, slides, readings and the case formulation open for them — but they are not certified and do not get Level 1 privileges until an admin passes their case formulation (or credits the level in Administration → Members).

How to join the community

Registration is free and open to everyone — no prior training required.

1

Register free — Level 0

Anyone interested can create an account and become a TIIP Trainee at Level 0: watch selected introductory modules, follow events, and get engaged. Level 0 members cannot take consultations and are not listed in the provider directory.

2

Progress through Levels 1–3

Complete the foundations (Level 1), intermediate training (Level 2), and supervised practice (Level 3 — 200 hours and 10 cases). Your position on the pathway is tracked in the member portal, and directory listing begins at Level 1.

3

Reach full certification

Fully certified clinicians gain complete clinical and consultation privileges — and eligibility for the TIIP Supervisor track.

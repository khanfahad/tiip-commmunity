What is TIIP?

A bridge between revelation and the clinic

TIIP is a holistic, structured psychotherapeutic framework that draws on classical Islamic understandings of the human soul (nafs) alongside modern psychological science — equipping clinicians to treat the whole person: mind, body, and spirit.

The framework at a glance

A model of the soul, a map for healing

TIIP understands the human being as an integrated whole composed of interacting spiritual faculties. Psychological distress is read not only through symptoms, but through the state of the heart, the strength of the intellect, and the discipline of the self.

From this map, the model derives a structured, replicable therapeutic process — one that honors the patient’s faith as a resource for resilience and transformation.

What is TIIP?

A bridge between revelation and the clinic

TIIP is situated within an Islamic epistemological and ontological framework — offering an account of the nature and composition of the human psyche, its drives, health, pathology, mechanisms of psychological change, and principles of healing. An integrative approach lets clinicians draw on empirically validated interventions, culturally and religiously adapted, alongside exploratory Islamic interventions of promising value.

Foundations of TIIP

The four cornerstones of the framework

From the Khalil Center Foundations of Traditional Islamically Integrated Psychotherapy training — the philosophical and clinical ground the model is built on.

📜

Epistemology — how we know

Three sources of knowledge held in equality: the senses (ḥissī), truthful reports (khabar — revelation and creation), and reason (ʿaql). Mental health sits at the meeting point of empirical, revelational, and rational knowledge.

🕌

Ontology — the human psyche

The human being is outer (ẓāhir) and inner (bāṭin): one subtle essence (al-laṭīfah al-rabbāniyyah) manifesting as heart (qalb), intellect (ʿaql), spirit (rūḥ), and inclination (nafs) — interacting, interchangeable, and interconnected.

🌿

Health — the schema of wellness

Health is more than symptom relief: proximity to God and His Messenger, God-consciousness (iḥsān), and a meaningful life tied to original purpose — melting bio-psycho-social-spiritual barriers along the way.

⚖️

Pathology — beyond the DSM

Illness is the maladaptive functioning of the psyche's elements — read on a continuum from clinical range to saintly rank, distinguishing exogenous and endogenous presentations after al-Balkhī, and weighing spiritual as well as clinical dysfunction.

The TIIP Certification Pathway

Registration is free and open to all. Every member starts as a TIIP Trainee at Level 0 and can progress — at their own pace — through three training levels to full certification.

  1. 0
    Register free

    Level 0 · TIIP Trainee

    For anyone interested and eager to be engaged. Watch selected introductory modules, follow community events, and explore the model.

    No consultations · not listed in the directory
  2. 1
    Foundations

    Level 1

    Complete the foundations coursework and assessment in the TIIP ontological framework.

    Directory listing begins
  3. 2
    Intermediate

    Level 2

    Advanced seminars, applied skills training, and entry into supervised practicum work.

  4. 3
    Supervised practice

    Level 3

    200 supervised practice hours and 10 completed cases, reviewed and approved by a TIIP supervisor.

  5. ✓
    Certified

    Full Certification

    Full clinical and consultation privileges, and eligibility for the TIIP Supervisor track.

The framework at a glance

A model of the soul, a map for healing

TIIP conceptualizes the psyche as interacting elements — cognition, spirit, inclination, and emotion — all feeding into the qalb, the heart. Distress is read not only through symptoms, but through the state of each element and how they fall in and out of balance.

From this map, the model derives a structured, four-stage process of change — one that honors the patient’s faith as a resource and aims at qalb salīm, the sound and healthy heart.

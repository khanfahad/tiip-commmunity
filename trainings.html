TIIP Trainings

The structured TIIP curriculum — courses and certification levels you work through at your own pace. Trainings are the coursework; Events are the dated cohorts, webinars, and gatherings where some of these trainings are delivered live.

The curriculum

From open registration to full certification

Every training below maps to a stage of the certification pathway. Start free at Level 0; higher levels unlock as you progress and are approved.

🎬
Level 0 · Open to all · Free

Six-Part Introductory Series

The open introductory series from Khalil Center — the foundation every member starts with. Self-paced video, roughly 3.5 hours total.

  • 1Introducing Islamically Integrated Psychotherapies
  • 2A Glimpse into the Living Islamic Tradition
  • 3Traditional Islamically Integrated Psychotherapy (TIIP)
  • 4TIIP Treatment of OCD Scrupulosity (Waswasa)
  • 5Dreams & Their Role in Islamically Integrated Practice
  • 6Islamic Jurisprudence & Mental Health Care

Level 1 · Foundations

Foundations of TIIP

Eight modules plus a required end-of-level TIIP Case Formulation Sheet, reviewed by a supervisor before Level 1 is credited.

  • ◈Foundations of TIIP & the role of the TIIP clinician
  • ◈Assessment & conceptualization
  • ◈The elements of the psyche — ʿaql, nafs, iḥsās, rūḥ
  • ◈Islamic virtues in clinical practice

Prerequisite: Level 0 · admin-approved enrollment.

Level 2 · Intermediate

Applied Skills & Seminars

Advanced seminars and applied skills training that deepen the model and open entry into supervised practicum work.

  • ◈Advanced intervention seminars across the four domains
  • ◈Applied skills labs & live demonstrations
  • ◈Practicum entry & supervision readiness

Prerequisite: completed Level 1.

Continue to Level 2 →
📙
Level 3 · Supervised Practice

Supervised Practicum

The final stage before certification: supervised clinical work logged and approved by an assigned TIIP supervisor.

  • ⏱200 supervised practice hours
  • 🗂10 completed cases, reviewed & approved
  • 🧭Ongoing case consultation & the Scholar Desk

Prerequisite: completed Level 2.

Progress to Level 3 →
Beyond certification

Full Certification & the TIIP Supervisor Track

Completing Level 3 confers full clinical and consultation privileges. Certified clinicians become eligible for the Supervisor / Scholar track — mentoring trainees, approving supervised hours, and reviewing case formulations for the next generation.

See the full certification pathway →
⚖️

Who these trainings are for: TIIP training is intended for those who are legally and ethically permitted to practice psychotherapy or mental-health counseling in their own jurisdiction. It is not a qualification for life-coaching, imam or chaplaincy guidance, or other non-clinical roles, and it does not by itself authorize anyone to practice psychotherapy.
